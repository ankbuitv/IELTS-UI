# Product roadmap — 50 feature suggestions

Ideas to grow Aieo into a full learning platform. Grouped by theme, roughly
ordered by impact inside each theme. Nothing here is implemented yet — this is
the brainstorm the team asked for, ready to pick from.

## Learning & practice (1–10)

1. **Adaptive practice sets** — after each attempt, auto-generate a 10-question set targeting the weakest question types (TFNG, matching headings, multiple choice…).
2. **Daily streaks + micro-lessons** — a 5-minute “word of the day”, one grammar bite, one listening cloze; streak freezes like Duolingo.
3. **Spaced-repetition vocabulary deck** — save unknown words from reading/ listening passages; review with an SRS scheduler (SM-2).
4. **Smart mock-test scheduler** — pick exam date → the app plans full mocks, section drills and rest days backwards from it.
5. **Question-type dojo** — drill a single type (e.g. map labelling) with instant feedback and a difficulty ladder.
6. **Dictation & shadowing mode** — play a sentence from a listening part, type what you hear, compare; shadowing recorder with waveform.
7. **Reading speed trainer** — timed passages with a words-per-minute tracker and comprehension checks.
8. **Handwriting/typing practice for Task 1 charts** — sentence builders for “the chart illustrates…”, trend verbs, linkers bank.
9. **Mistake journal** — every wrong answer lands in a personal journal with the explanation, your note and a “retest in 7 days” tick.
10. **Offline/PWA mode** — cache today’s planned drill so practice works on flaky connections.

## Speaking & writing coaching (11–20)

11. **AI speaking partner** — voice conversation with follow-up questions, then a band-style feedback card (fluency, lexical, grammar, pronunciation).
12. **Pronunciation heatmap** — per-phoneme accuracy from the transcript, minimal-pair drills for the sounds that hurt.
13. **Writing task 2 essay templates** — interactive outlines (opinion/discussion/advantages) with drag-in linkers and a timer.
14. **Paragraph rewriter** — paste a paragraph, get 3 rewrites at higher band with the diffs highlighted.
15. **Cohesion checker** — underline cohesive devices, pronoun references and topic sentences in the submitted essay.
16. **Collocation suggestions** — while typing, suggest strong collocations (“pose a threat” not “make a threat”).
17. **Speaking part 2 cue-card simulator** — 1 min prep notepad with countdown + 2 min recording + self-rating rubric.
18. **Recording comparison** — side-by-side playback of your first and latest take of the same cue card, with waveform and duration.
19. **Teacher video feedback** — a mentor records a 2-min Loom-style voice/video note on an essay or speaking take.
20. **Vocabulary range meter** — type-token ratio + academic word list coverage trend per essay over time.

## Mentor & classroom (21–30)

21. **Mentor marketplace** — public mentor profiles with intro video, pricing, calendar, specialties, verified badge and reviews.
22. **Recurring class sessions** — a class’s weekly slot auto-creates the room, reminders and attendance each week.
23. **Attendance & participation log** — who joined the class call, for how long, hand-raises and chat activity.
24. **Class whiteboard** — shared drawing/text board inside the call room (even a simple sticky-note board helps).
25. **Breakout rooms** — split a class call into pairs for speaking tasks, then a timer pulls everyone back.
26. **Homework board per class** — mentor posts a task with a due date; members submit (essay/recording) and get feedback.
27. **Mentor earnings dashboard** — sessions taught, coins earned, payout requests, monthly chart.
28. **Booking packages** — buy 4/8/12 sessions upfront with a discount and a priority booking window.
29. **Mentor–student shared notes** — a living doc per pair: goals, band targets, vocabulary to watch.
30. **Substitute/co-mentor** — a class can add a second mentor with the same chat/call powers.

## Assessment & analytics (31–38)

31. **Predicted band widget** — confidence band with trend line and “what to do next” action cards.
32. **Skill radar over time** — animated radar chart per month (reading/listening/writing/speaking).
33. **Time-per-question analytics** — spot the question types where time bleeds away.
34. **Cohort benchmarks** — anonymised “you are faster than 62% of Band 6.5 candidates” style insights.
35. **Readiness score for exam date** — % chance of hitting target band based on mock history; red-amber-green.
36. **Auto-generated weekly report** — email/push every Sunday: minutes practiced, band movement, next week’s plan.
37. **Export PDF report** — one-page progress PDF for parents/schools with charts.
38. **Custom scoring profiles** — teachers set class-specific scoring (e.g. strict Task 2 criteria) for their assignments.

## Gamification & motivation (39–44)

39. **League seasons** — weekly bronze→silver→gold leagues by XP from practice minutes and scores.
40. **Achievement badges** — first full mock, 7-day streak, Band 7 club, 10 essays, speaking marathon…
41. **Quests & challenges** — “Complete 3 reading drills this week” with coin rewards.
42. **Class leaderboard** — friendly ranking inside a classroom by practice minutes (opt-outable).
43. **Reward shop** — spend coins on discount coupons, extra mock reviews, mentor AMA tickets.
44. **Countdown widget to exam day** — on the dashboard, with a motivational tip each morning.

## Social & communication (45–50)

45. **Study groups** — student-created groups with their own chat + call (reuse the class room stack).
46. **Forum / Q&A board** — ask about a question type, mentors answer, upvotes, “accepted answer”.
47. **Voice messages in chat** — 30-second voice notes in class/DM chat for pronunciation Q&A.
48. **Push & email notifications** — session reminders, class call starting, mentor replied, streak at risk.
49. **Parent/guardian view** — a read-only progress link parents can open (weekly report, attendance).
50. **Localization (vi/en) + dark mode** — full Vietnamese UI, theme switcher, per-user preference.

---

*Pick any slice, size it (S/M/L), and turn it into an issue. Items that reuse
existing building blocks: 21–22 (mentor stack), 23–26 (class room + chat),
45–47 (same), 31–32 (charts components), 40–43 (gamification tables).*
